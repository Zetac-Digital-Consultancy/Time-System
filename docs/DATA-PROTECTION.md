# Operator information required before onboarding

This is a preparation checklist, not a published privacy notice.

- Controller's legal name, address and privacy contact for each company.
- Platform operator's legal name, address and privacy/security contact.
- Purpose and lawful basis for processing employee identity, work times, sites and audit records.
- Hosting provider, hosting location, subprocessors and processing agreements.
- Who may access employee records, and how access is granted, reviewed and revoked.
- Retention periods for timesheets, accounts, notifications, audit/authentication logs and backups; exceptions for legally required retention.
- Process for employee information/access/correction requests and applicable deletion requests.
- Incident detection, escalation contacts, assessment and response procedure.
- Public-facing legal/privacy information required for the intended service and jurisdiction.

The app does not publish invented company details or automatically delete employee records. Complete and review the actual notice and retention policy before collecting production data.
